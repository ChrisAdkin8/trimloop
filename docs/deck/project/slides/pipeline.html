Today’s prototype

Trimloop turns your logs into advice

Most tools we have seen show where the money went. Trimloop adds why, and what to change.

1 Read

The logs Claude Code keeps on your machine

2 Build

Your sessions, turn by turn

3 Price

Each turn at Anthropic’s published prices

4 Detect

Nine kinds of waste

5 Advise

Fixes, ranked by money saved

6 Report

A summary, a full report, or data for scripts

It recommends. It does not change anything for you.

The next two slides open up steps 4 and 5. One command, trimloop, runs all six steps. The logs live in ~/.claude/projects. Nothing is sent anywhere. The short summary is the default; --open shows the full report as a web page, and --format json gives every figure for scripts.
